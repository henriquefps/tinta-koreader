Tinta: dashboard prototype

A KOReader plugin that turns the Kindle into an app dashboard. The screen is shown at the real size of the Kindle KT3 (600 × 800 px, black and white), with the apps the public Tinta ships: Notifications, System, Battery, Clock, Sleep, Timer, Note and Countdown, plus three sample apps for developers (marked "sample"). All data is made up.

How to use

  • Tap the widgets, the bell, the clock or the bottom bar, as on the device.
  • Menu › Apps opens the launcher; Menu › Organize home opens the organizer.
  • URL parameters: ?view=home|yourapps|central|apps|sleep|sistema|sleepcfg|organizar|relogio|timer|nota|contagem|bateria|clima|servidor|modelo, &land=1, &style=analogico, &samples=1 (list the sample apps in Apps) and &shot=1 (only the screen, in real px, for the screenshots).
  • The time is fixed (Friday, October 16, 14:32) so the screenshots are reproducible.
